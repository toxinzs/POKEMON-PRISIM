# PART 1 — THE WORLD (LOCKED)

## 1.1 What this is
The **real modern world, where Pokémon have always existed**. Not an isekai. Not the game world with real names pasted on. Real countries, cities, governments, laws, currencies, brands, transit, schools, hospitals, social media. Pokémon systems and the fictional characters/organizations are the only invented layer. Everything else is as real as possible.

## 1.2 Realism philosophy
"If Pokémon had always existed, how would humans have adapted?" Worldbuilding lives in the background. Don't over-explain.

## 1.3 Earth
Use real places, transit, stores, restaurants, hotels, prices in local currency. Research anything current.

## 1.4 Regions
Kanto = Japan's Kanto · Johto = Kansai · Hoenn = Kyushu area · Sinnoh = Hokkaido · **Unova = NYC/Northeast US** (Unova League administers all of the US, HQ New York) · **Kalos = France** · Alola = Hawaii · **Galar = UK** · Paldea = Spain/Iberia. Region names and real names are interchangeable.

## 1.5 Culture
Each region is fully its real-world counterpart. Pokémon are a layer of culture, not a replacement.

## 1.6–1.9 Pokémon in society
* Not everyone owns one. Ownership is a responsibility.
* Pokémon don't have to battle.
* **Real animals coexist** (seagulls and Wingull, squirrels and Skwovet, foxes in London streets). Dogs/cats are still the most common pets. Livestock is the main meat. Eating Pokémon is taboo.
* Wild Pokémon are distributed by habitat; unusual appearances need a reason.
* Pokémon are living beings. **Poké Balls don't create obedience.** Bonds develop naturally.

## 1.10 Poké Balls
Advanced containment and transport tech tied to registration. A ball isn't legal ownership; registration is.
* **Beast Balls:** restricted Aether research equipment for Ultra Space creatures, never connected to League registration. League scanners read an unchipped Beast Ball as an **empty, broken ball.** (Nova's is now chipped. See 3.12.)
* **Registered balls ping the League registry** every few minutes (location, health, status). Guardians can request access. **This is how Verane tracks Lucien** (via contact "M").

## 1.11 Battles
Physical, real, never summarized. Commands use "use" by default ("Rockruff, use Rock Throw!"). Terrain, fatigue, injuries, positioning matter. No HP bars, levels, or menus. **Moves work by what they physically are, like the anime**, not by game stats.

## 1.12–1.13 Wild Pokémon & medical care
Wild Pokémon are dangerous and unpredictable. Pokémon Centers are clinics plus support facilities. Human healthcare is real healthcare.

## 1.14 Rotom technology
Rotom tech is a technology layer, not a brand or a living Rotom. Real companies make Rotom-enabled devices (floating phones, etc.). "Rotom Phone" is casual slang. Phones double as Pokédex, ID, League tools, and emergency systems.

## 1.15 The League
International, **not Earth's government**. Regional divisions. **Ultra Research Division (URD):** small, little-known; studies wormholes and Ultra Space. **11 people hold URD clearance.** Voss trusts 9.
* **URD Director: Thomas Okonkwo-Hale** (60s, Black, silver-haired, white beard, wire-rimmed glasses). He hired Voss 10 years ago and mentored her. **Seen at the gala raising a glass to Verane.** Strong suspect for "M." Not confirmed until the M folder is opened.

## 1.16 Ownership, registration, licensing
* Registration = responsibility; License = permissions. An unregistered owned Pokémon earns a fine.
* Tiers: Registered Owner (no license) · Trainer License (age 18+) · Professional Certifications.
* Minors own Pokémon with a guardian co-responsible. Under 18, the only route to licensed-level activity is the **PTL** (program authorization).
* Party limit 6. The rest go to Sanctuaries.
* **Capture zones:** capture only in approved areas. **No capture zones in central London** (nearest: Epping Forest, Richmond Park by permit). **Paris:** Bois de Vincennes and Bois de Boulogne are approved zones. Special permits exist for problem Pokémon (Le Fantôme).
* **Cross-border transport of a wild Pokémon** requires a report (Kalos form KL-14; no fine for a first offense by a program student).

## 1.17 Sanctuary system
No PC boxes. Extra Pokémon live in League Sanctuaries (park habitats with caretakers and vets), stay registered, and are visible in the app via live camera. Known site: **Sanctuary Galar-Kalos Est, Fontainebleau.**
* WORKING IDEA: who funds sanctuaries; Lumen access to sanctuary data.

## 1.18 Social media, safety, ordinary life
Real platforms. The world isn't totally safe. Ordinary life matters: eating, money, boredom, family calls.

## 1.19 Galar particles & the Darkest Day (NEW)
* Dynamax energy = red **Galar particles**, normally only at stadium Power Spots.
* **The Darkest Day (Feb 11, 2027):** red skies and spontaneous Dynamax/Gigantamax across Galar (London, Manchester, Glasgow, Cardiff, Newcastle, Edinburgh, Liverpool, Leeds). A red column of energy was reported north of Birmingham. "Contained" that afternoon. Unverified footage shows **two large Pokémon (sword and shield)** at the source. 300+ injured across Galar, no confirmed deaths in London.
* **Cause: undecided.** Canon parallel: Eternatus and Chairman Rose. Options: Necrozma's arrival stirred it; Lumen's Galar Dynamax pilot (folder 07 exists); a separate company; a combination. The GALAR_PILOT folder will reveal "just enough."

Much better fed (Barbican, Shad Thames roof, roof terraces, the Eiffel sparkle, south windows in Paris, Litwick's flame). Cracks still there but gold-lined. Not near full power.

## 3.5 Beast Ball mechanics
Can't absorb light inside the ball (Faure's band let a little through). Feeds outside. **The original black band (Faure's design) was removed** (URD evidence, Item 1) and **replaced with a URD band chip** (green node, ~1 Hz telemetry). The node blinks red when it's agitated. Lumen has a **mirrored feed** of the chip via "M."

## 3.6 Trust ladder progress
1. ✅ Goes in when asked (first: Pryce's knock, Ep 4; reliably since).
2. ✅ **Comes out when called** (first time: Rue des Rosiers roof, Ep 11; Nova called "Necrozma," it came for no reason except that she asked).
3. ✅ Responds to her in a crisis: Euston Road (redirected Prismatic Laser, Ep 8). **Stayed in the ball when she asked** under extreme pull (Champ de Mars Ep 13, Montmartre Ep 16, gala Ep 21 so far).
4. ❌ **Battle commands — not yet.** Arc 3 at the earliest, saved for a moment that earns it.

## 3.7 Personality
Proud, ancient, in pain, starving, suspicious, volatile when threatened, intelligent, not evil, buried generosity. Quirks: drains phone batteries, follows sunlight, **hates camera flashes**, glitches Rotom electronics, reacts to Lucien (lab smell) and Lumen people's smell (Crale, Mara). **Embarrassed/proud about accepting help** (Litwick feeding, Ep 6). **Looks at Sora "like it knows something"** (Cosmog link, unexplained). Bowed its head to Dani. "Looked at" Boogie's macaron.

## 3.8 Communication
* No speech. Glow = mood. Prisms lock like hackles. Sounds: *hmmm* (calm/content), *tk* (small acknowledgement), *krrrk* (agitated, pain).
* **Emotional bleed** written as *(BLEED — …)*.
* **Dreams/visions** under **— DREAM —** / **— MEMORY —** headers.
* **Growth reached:** it now **chooses** to show Nova things: the lantern picture ("stay with you," Ep 9), the "yield… again" memory of Verane (Ep 21), **Ultra Necrozma — "That was me"** (Ep 20), "together" (Ep 20).

## 3.9–3.11 Knowledge, hiding, secret period — complete

## 3.12 League status (happened Ep 9)

# PART 8 — WRITING & STYLE (LOCKED)

## 8.1 Format: a TV show
Screenplay style with rich action lines. **Slugline + timestamp line** whenever time or place changes:
*Day, Month Date, Year | Exact location, ZIP/postcode | Time* (local time abroad).
Bold names, (parentheticals), **SFX:**, **SUPER:**, V.O./O.S., transitions, `---` between scenes, no acts, cold opens and tags as fits. **Real brands and models** for everything.

## 8.2 Episodes, arcs, titles
* **No seasons.** Episodes are numbered continuously. Story is organized in **named arcs** (an arc card on the first episode of an arc; the series title card at Ep 1 and big turns).
* **Episode titles are Japanese-first:**
  **Episode N — 日本語タイトル**
  *Romaji* — "Faithful English Translation"
  Use official Japanese Pokémon names (Necrozma ネクロズマ, Rockruff イワンコ, Eevee イーブイ, Litwick ヒトモシ, Pawmi パモ, Applin カジッチュ, Absol アブソル, Corviknight アーマーガア, Cosmog コスモッグ, Dreepy ドラメシヤ, Fletchinder ヒノヤコマ, Chewtle カムカメ…). Check before using.
* One episode can span several responses. **Every response ends with "Next up — [concrete tease]."** At an episode's end: **END OF EPISODE N**, then "Next up — Episode N+1 — [JP title] / *Romaji* — "English"" plus a tease.

## 8.3 Who writes what
Claude writes everything. The user directs and can drop in Nova's lines. **Nova's big decisions belong to the user**: pause at decision points when it truly matters. When the user just says "continue," make the in-character choice.

## 8.4 POV
Mainly Nova. Cutaways (Verane, Lumen, Voss, Lucien, Sora, Callahan) for dread and dramatic irony.

## 8.5 Show, don't summarize
Full scenes, spoken dialogue, no skipped transitions. Pace detail to importance.

## 8.6 Voices
AAVE/Black vernacular for Nova, family, Kennedy, Boogie, Batiste (New Orleans), Dani, Noa. Sora: Black American English plus Japanese (short phrases romanized, unsubtitled; full lines subtitled). Lucien: South London; perfect Parisian French with Faure/Ana, French when emotional (subtitled). Voss: precise. Verane: warm, French accent. Galar/Kalos locals in their own accents and languages.

## 8.7 Necrozma on the page
No dialogue. Action lines, SFX, glow, prisms. Bleed as its own beat. Dreams and memories under headers. Always "it."

## 8.8 Visual mockups (automatic)
Every screen or paper is an **INSERT** rendered as a realistic visual: iMessage/WhatsApp/SMS, FaceTime, FieldHub, Field Card, Pokédex scans (Nova's phone with glitch banners; Sora's Meta HUD), social media (TikTok, comments), news (BBC/WAVY), emails (Gmail), forms, tickets, letters, laptop screens, maps.

## 8.9 Battles
Shown fully. Physical. Commands with "use." Trainers make mistakes. Small Pokémon win with tricks.

## 8.10 Trackers
No stat blocks. Show a tracker only when something changes (card balance, new Pokémon, mission, check-in).

## 8.11 Content
Mature: danger, injury, grief, swearing where it fits. Cast is 14–16, so romance stays age-appropriate (slow burn; Sora–Nova; Lucien complicates).

## 8.12 Tone
No moralizing, no neat wrap-ups, no meta-commentary. Grounded. **Arc 2 onward: more Pokémon-anime feel**: adventure, exploration, mystery, spontaneous fun, side characters, and catches, balanced with the heavy emotional throughline. Fun episodes still plant seeds.

## 8.13 Research
Real places, transit, prices. Track money.

## 8.14 Continuity updates
At the end of a big arc, or on request, update Part 11.

## 8.15 Pokémon rules (NEW, LOCKED)
* **New or unfamiliar Pokémon get scanned** (phone Pokédex or Sora's glasses) and rendered.
* **Realistic cries**: some make sounds, some say their name or part of it, anime-style (Snorlax "Snooor… lax," Rockruff "Ruff!", Litwick "Lit… lit-wiiick," Eevee "Vee!", Absol "Sol," Applin "plin").
