# POKÉMON: PRISM — Pacing Map (rough, not locked canon)

Last updated Oct 8, 2026 in the planning chat. Arc positions, not episode numbers, since arc lengths aren't set. The show is expected to run 100+ episodes, so nothing should be rushed. For older ideas that didn't make it into the continuity guide, see the earlier planning chat ("Pokémon and real animals rules").

## Pacing rules we agreed on
- No evolutions in Arc 2.
- Evolutions are about 30–50 episodes apart by default. Some Pokémon can evolve sooner if it depends on the Pokémon and it really needs it (see "Early-evolution exceptions"). Annihilape isn't level-based, so the spacing doesn't apply to it.
- Teams of 3–4 Pokémon. About 5 moves max per Pokémon (soft ceiling). Dim is not an official move, just something Litwick can do.
- Moves can be "revealed" from moves a Pokémon already knows (e.g. Ember turning out to be something bigger), which swaps a move instead of adding one.
- Characters don't need to plan, know about, or count toward evolutions (anime style). No Rage Fist counting for Sora.
- Focus episodes put one Pokémon in the spotlight, and something changes for it (an evolution, a new strategy, a new move).
- Nova gets her next Pokémon before Sora does.
- Sora tries to catch every new Pokémon he sees and doesn't care about catch zones ("gray areas" get him warnings).
- Arc 3 should restore the program/mission structure (missions have been slipping).

## Early-evolution exceptions
- Can evolve sooner than the default spacing: Popplio → Brionne (the evolution fixes its popping balloons), Litwick → Lampent (tiny and fragile, and it must come before Goodra), Mankey → Primeape (happens in the sanctuary before Sora owns it), and side-cast Pokémon like Journee's Pichu, Kennedy's Riolu, and Jax's Pawmi.
- Keep the full spacing: Iwanko → Dusk Lycanroc (the series' first evolution), Sliggoo → Goodra (rain scene and storm-fear payoff), Eevee (it decides for itself), and Brionne → Primarina and Lampent → Chandelure (the big finishes).
- Keep it to about one early evolution per arc so it stays an exception.

## Arc 3: Kanto (Sora's spotlight)
- Just Sora and Nova, no Batiste (she stays in Paris with Wren). Lucien around loosely.
- Early: Sora meets Mankey, tries to catch it and fails. They become running partners.
- Mid: the Tama Hills sanctuary storyline (Mankey is a ward, adoption is a process, a mission to outdo Mankey, a battle catch). Mankey evolves into Primeape in a focus episode, before Sora owns it. The adoption finalizes in a later arc.
- Mid (Nova's catch): Popplio arrives as a sanctuary transfer from an Oʻahu sanctuary where Papa K's family works. It doesn't fit in there (its bubble balloon keeps popping), and like Litwick it chooses Nova. Needs League registration, so Hale and the URD chip thread stay in play. Papa K calls about where it came from. Nova gets it before Sora's Mankey catch is finished.
- Late: Iwanko (Own Tempo) evolves into Dusk Lycanroc at sunset on Mt. Takao, after Sora pushes it and it resists. The series' first evolution. It happens where the 2019 aperture opened, but it is Sora's moment, not something Hale sets up.
- Cosmog thread: Takao 2019 and K-19-0812. Show who wants it and what happened, no species named yet.
- Working arc titles from the guide: "Falling Stars," "Where It Fell," "Cedar and Starlight."

## Later arcs (order not fixed, nothing rushed)
- Alola arc (full): Nova's family and Necrozma lore, Z-Moves for Sora (seeded). Brionne should land around here, or sooner.
- Hokkaido / Sinnoh (Hisui): Nova's alpha Hisuian Sliggoo, Sora's shiny Hisuian Braviary, Mankey adoption finalizes. This can move later, no rush.
- Kalos return: Mega Evolution mini-arc for Nova's Chandelure, with a Key Stone and a possible run of failures before it works. Voss's Key Stone was confiscated, so the stone has to come from somewhere else.
- Possible in-between "time skip" arc (half Nova, half Sora) showing how they changed. Not next.

## Evolution order (working)
1. Iwanko → Dusk Lycanroc (late Arc 3).
2. Litwick → Lampent (must come before Goodra). No stone needed. Can come sooner than the default.
3. Popplio → Brionne, can come sooner than the default.
4. Sliggoo → Goodra, 30–50 episodes after that catch, in rain (rain and fog both, per Mason).
5. Brionne → Primarina and Lampent → Chandelure (needs a Dusk Stone), much later. Mega Chandelure after.
- Chandelure is probably not Nova's last evolution now (Primarina and Goodra may land after it). Exact order still open.
- Eevee's evolution (Sora) is its own choice, well after Iwanko's, with its own focus episode. Umbreon was floated earlier, not locked.
- Annihilape: no schedule.

## Endgame idea (working, not canon) — from the earlier planning chat
- Sora's Cosmog becomes Solgaleo. Solgaleo recognizes Sora and travels with him for a while. Solgaleo near Necrozma is exactly what Verane wants (Dusk Mane fusion), so Lucien catches it to separate them. Sora is furious, then understands, and learns to let go of what he wanted most. Sora gets the moment, Lucien gets the ball.
