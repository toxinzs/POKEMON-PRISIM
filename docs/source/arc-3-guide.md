ARC 3 DIRECTION: KANTO

THE SETUP
- The Kanto trip is the field mission already posted to their FieldHub: MSN-KT-0311, the Kanto Spring Field Survey at Takao and the Tama Hills. They report to Takao Ranger Station in Hachiōji, under Ranger Chief Y. Kobayashi. It runs about five weeks, starting March 20. Pay is ¥60,000 and a Ranger field certification (Lv1). Departure from Paris is no earlier than March 15, so Paris wraps up before then.
- The survey includes a status report on the Tama Hills Pokémon Sanctuary ward program.
- Nova's trip is approved under the amended URD-CR-1. Okonkwo-Hale is her interim liaison. She has a daily 9 AM Paris video check-in with him, both cameras on, with everyone in the room named. Firmware v3.0 is installed, and the ball's pulse changed after the update. Dani's margin conditions still apply: a vet chosen only by Batiste, a call home every four hours, and any change after the firmware goes straight to a review panel under Article Eleven. Keep the time difference between Japan and Paris in mind.
- Batiste stays in Paris. Wren needs someone to stay with her, and Batiste still has the gala fallout to deal with: the Aether inquiry, statements to the League, and Voss's suspension. She says goodbye to Sora and Nova at the station, and she stays reachable by phone.
- It is Sora and Nova. Lucien has no license and a stolen Hisuian Zoroark, so he moves carefully. He can show up in Kanto but isn't on the mission.
- Bring back the program. Give the episodes real missions and field work, each with a clear job that also pushes the main plot.
- Hale wanted them at Takao. In 2019, an Ultra Space aperture opened on Mt. Takao, and Voss's notebook lists Hale as field lead of the team that went in. The Aether file K-19-0812 and the August 12, 2019 photo in Sora's father's box tie into that night. Everyone in the group knows this is a trap, and they go anyway.
- Keep these threads open: Hale's real motive, Voss, Necrozma's conditional registration, the telemetry, and Faure's drive folders 06 and 07. Don't resolve any casually.
- Nothing gets rushed. Catches and evolutions are rare and earned.

SORA
- Sora tries to catch every new Pokémon he sees and doesn't care about capture zones. Unlisted areas are "gray areas" to him, and that causes trouble. Wild Pokémon in Kanto should keep tempting him.
- Early: he meets a wild Mankey and tries to catch it right away. It doesn't work. They start running together instead.
- Mid-arc: the Tama Hills sanctuary storyline. Mankey is a ward there and adoption is a process, with a mission to outdo Mankey and a battle catch folded in. In an episode focused on Mankey, it evolves into Primeape in the sanctuary, before Sora owns it. The adoption isn't finished by the end of the arc.
- Late arc: Iwanko evolves into Dusk Lycanroc at sunset on Mt. Takao. Sora pushes, Iwanko resists, and then it happens. It is the series' first evolution and the emotional peak of Sora's arc. It happens on the ground where the 2019 aperture opened, but it is Sora's and Iwanko's moment, not something Hale sets up.
- The glowing light that led Sora out of Takao in 2019 is a real, living thing that someone wanted. Show who wants it and what happened to it, but don't name the species yet.
- Sprigatito goes to a sanctuary. Sora gives it to Journee, so Meowscarada can show up and grow later.
- Eevee gets small moments only.
- No one counts evolutions or talks about plans for them.

NOVA
- Her development is mostly small: Litwick's tactics, remote advice from Marchand, and Necrozma trusting her a little more. Litwick's moves are Ember, Astonish, Smog, and Minimize.
- Mid-arc she gets a Popplio, before Sora finishes catching Mankey. It arrives as a transfer from a sanctuary on Oʻahu, where Papa K's family works. It doesn't fit in there: its bubble balloon keeps popping and it keeps getting passed over. Papa K arranges the transfer after reading Nova's note, and he knows Encounter Day left her with no Pokémon. Treat it as routine program work through the Tama Hills sanctuary, but the Popplio doesn't go to anyone automatically. Like Litwick, it chooses Nova.
- Registering it needs League paperwork, which means going through Hale. Papa K calls to explain where it came from. Dani and Article Eleven still apply to it.
- Popplio's early moves and Brionne are for later. Don't evolve it in this arc.
